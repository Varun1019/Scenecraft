# Scenecraft Community Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, constructive, and harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to or using Scenecraft, regardless of background, identity, or technical experience.

## Standards & Expectations

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community:
- Being respectful, patient, and collaborative.
- Providing thoughtful, actionable feedback on issues and pull requests.
- Keeping bug reports and discussions focused on the software and technical facts.
- Respecting user privacy: never posting private keys, credentials, or personal information.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Insulting, derogatory comments, or personal attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement & Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via the contact details provided in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
