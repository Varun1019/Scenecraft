# Scenecraft Visual Style Bible: Earthy Explainer Preset

## 1. Aesthetic Identity
- **Genre**: Hand-drawn 2D cartoon illustration, graphic-novel explainer aesthetic.
- **Inspiration**: Viral animated documentary and survival explainers.
- **Core Visual Pillars**:
  1. **Thick Dark Ink Outlines**: Clean, consistent contour lines on characters, props, and architectural lines.
  2. **Muted Earthy Color Palette**: Dominant grey concrete (`#8C8D8A`), dusty brown (`#8D775F`), olive green (`#5F6B4E`), and warm mustard yellow (`#D4A017`).
  3. **Protagonist**: The Wanderer (retained identity: brick-red knit beanie, mustard-and-maroon striped scarf, warm ivory oval head, thin black stick limbs).
  4. **Lighting & Texture**: Soft cinematic lighting, gentle 2D cel-shading, and tactile paper-like surface texture.
  5. **Dynamic Shot Variety**: Balances detailed atmospheric cinematic scenes with clean infographic explanatory object cards.

---

## 2. Shot Typologies & Composition

| Shot Type | Composition & Purpose | Background Style |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Environmental Establishing** | Wide cinematic shot showing vast scale, decayed skyscrapers, rooftops, weather | Atmospheric muted concrete & dusty brown environment |
| **2. Full-Body Character Scene** | Wanderer navigating terrain, climbing ladders, carrying supplies | Fully illustrated realistic environment |
| **3. Facial Close-Up** | Tight focus on large ivory oval head, pill eyes, and beanie | Shallow depth of field, soft cel-shaded background |
| **4. Medium Character Shot** | Waist-level framing, character cooking, using tools, or signaling | Midground character interaction with props |
| **5. Object-Focused Illustration** | Tight focal shot on survival gear (camping stove, radio, water bottles) | Detailed technical rendering with ink hatching |
| **6. Architectural Cutaway** | Cross-section diagram of building floors, roof rope pulley system | Technical architectural drawing with clean ink lines |
| **7. Explanatory Card (White Card)** | Central illustrated object with bold handwritten heading and callouts | Clean off-white paper parchment background (`#F7F4EE`) |
| **8. Cinematic Atmospheric Scene** | Dusk/dawn skyline, light beams through dust motes, storm clouds | Rich gradient lighting with subtle paper grain |

---

## 3. Character Preservation (The Wanderer)
- **Head**: Large smooth warm ivory oval head (`#F4EBD9`).
- **Face**: Two minimalist vertical black pill-shaped eyes, simple neutral horizontal slit mouth. No nose, no hair.
- **Headwear**: Chunky brick-red ribbed knit beanie (`#A03325`) with folded brim.
- **Scarf**: Warm winter scarf with alternating mustard-gold (`#D4A017`) and rust-maroon (`#8C2B1D`) horizontal stripes with fringe.
- **Torso & Limbs**: Dark charcoal short-sleeved t-shirt (`#3D3A37`) over thin black ink stick arms and legs.
- **Rule**: Do not substitute or alter the Wanderer's character design.

---

## 4. Prompt Compilation Blueprint for Flow / Nano Banana
Each prompt must include:
1. **Art Style Header**: `Hand-drawn 2D cartoon illustration, graphic-novel and animated explainer aesthetic. Thick clean dark ink outlines, muted earthy palette (grey concrete, dusty brown, olive green, mustard yellow), soft cinematic lighting, gentle cel shading, subtle paper-like texture.`
2. **Shot Framing & Camera**: Explicit shot type (Wide establishing, macro close-up, explanatory card, etc.).
3. **Character Description**: Exact Wanderer anchor when present.
4. **Pose & Expression**: Action-driven, tension-calibrated body posture.
5. **Environment & Background Details**: Specific props, architecture, materials, and textures.
6. **Lighting & Color Distribution**: Directional light, shadow contrast, and color accents.
7. **Negative Constraints**: Strictly no borders, frames, speech bubbles, or 3D photorealism.
