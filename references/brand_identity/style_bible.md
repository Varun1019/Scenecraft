# Scenecraft Official Brand Identity & Style Bible

## 1. Character Overview: "The Wanderer" / "Red Beanie Stickman"
This document establishes the permanent, non-negotiable visual identity for Scenecraft's primary protagonist across all YouTube long-form videos, Shorts, thumbnails, and channel branding. 

The character is an iconic 2D dark comic stickman defined by an oversized ivory oval head, a brick-red ribbed knit beanie, a signature gold-and-maroon striped scarf, a dark charcoal t-shirt, and clean black stick limbs.

---

## 2. Anatomical & Visual Specifications

### A. Head & Facial Features
- **Head Shape**: Oversized smooth oval/egg shape with a gently flattened chin.
- **Head Color**: Warm pale ivory/cream (`#F9E8CE`), never harsh stark `#FFFFFF`.
- **Eyes**: Two vertical solid black rounded pill-shaped dots (`#18181A`). Centered vertically on the face, spaced with balanced pupil distance.
- **Mouth**: Minimalist single horizontal black slit (`-`). Expression changes through tilt, curve, or width:
  - Neutral: straight short horizontal bar.
  - Suspense / Fear: slightly wavy, thin, or small open oval gasp.
  - Panic: wider open mouth with dark interior.
- **Nose / Ears / Hair**: None. Smooth clean surface tucked under the beanie.

### B. Headwear: Brick-Red Knit Beanie
- **Style**: Chunky winter watch cap / knit beanie with folded ribbed brim.
- **Color**: Deep brick red / crimson rust (`#A03325`).
- **Fit**: Snug across the forehead, pulled slightly back, covering the upper half of the head.
- **Texture**: Clean segmented vertical ribbing notches along the folded brim.

### C. Neckwear: Striped Knitted Scarf
- **Style**: Thick, cozy double-wrapped winter muffler with dangling fringed end.
- **Pattern**: Distinct horizontal alternating stripes:
  - **Stripe A**: Warm mustard gold / amber ochre (`#DFA028`).
  - **Stripe B**: Deep rust maroon / dark brick red (`#8C2B1D`).
- **Detail**: Fine dark knit hatch marks across stripes; short dark tassel fringe at the tail.

### D. Torso & Attire
- **Garment**: Dark slate-charcoal short-sleeved crewneck t-shirt (`#454242`).
- **Cut**: Loose, slightly boxy rectangular silhouette ending at hip level.
- **Sleeves**: Short sleeves extending halfway down the upper stick arms.

### E. Limbs & Extremities
- **Arms & Legs**: Uniform thin solid black stick lines (`#18181A`).
- **Hands**: Simplified solid black rounded oval/mitten nubs. When interacting with props (flashlight, phone, weapon), fingers form a clean simplified grasp.
- **Feet**: Small horizontal rounded black pill feet.
- **Shadow**: Subtle oval ground drop shadow beneath feet for grounding.

## 4. Illustration Technique & Linework Rules

1. **Linework**:
   - Bold, confident, uniform black vector-style strokes (approx. 3-4px relative weight).
   - Clean continuous outlines; no sketchiness, no broken pencil scratches.
2. **Shading**:
   - Subtle 2D cel-shading.
   - Soft directional shadow cast by the beanie brim onto the upper forehead.
   - Soft shadow under the scarf collar onto the t-shirt.
   - Clean flat fills inside line boundaries with zero bleed or muddy gradients.
3. **Composition & Framing**:
   - Full-bleed edge-to-edge artwork.
   - Zero white borders, zero decorative frames, zero picture-in-picture padding, zero letterbox bars.
   - Cinematic background integration: rich moody lighting (night, mist, street lamps, flashlights) casts subtle rim light onto the character without compromising the iconic color fills.

---

## 5. Genre Adaptability Guidelines

- **Horror & Thrillers**:
  - Eyes widen slightly or narrow in dread; mouth opens to small oval gasp.
  - High-contrast directional lighting (flashlight beam, emergency strobe, moonlit mist) casts sharp dramatic shadows.
  - Attire stays identical; rain drops or subtle dust can gather on the beanie.
- **Survival & Apocalypse**:
  - Scarf can be wrapped tighter; character crouches or holds survival gear (backpack, radio, flashlight).
  - T-shirt can show subtle wear/scuffs when story demands, but beanie and striped scarf remain permanent identifiers.
- **Sci-Fi & Mystery**:
  - Glowing terminals, neon signs, and holographic screens cast vibrant cyan/amber colored rim lights across the dark t-shirt and beanie brim.

---

## 6. Official Negative Prompts & Prohibitions

- NO generic plain white round heads without beanie/scarf.
- NO thin wire stick figures without clothes.
- NO photorealistic 3D human faces or flesh textures.
- NO anime hair tufts peeking out (keep clean smooth forehead under beanie).
- NO different colored beanies (strictly brick-red/crimson).
- NO different colored scarves (strictly mustard-gold and rust-maroon stripes).
- NO borders, frames, white margins, letterboxing, or collage multi-panels.
